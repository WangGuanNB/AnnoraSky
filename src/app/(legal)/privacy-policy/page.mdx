# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 31, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how Annora Sky ("Annora Sky," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use [https://annorasky.com](https://annorasky.com) and related services (the "Service").

## What Annora Sky Provides

Annora Sky is a personal astrology product that lets you create a Western tropical birth chart from your birth date, time, and place; view placements, houses, aspects, and current transit highlights; and save chart profiles in your browser for later use. Interpretive text is generated from documented astrology rules and automated language systems.

## Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use the Service, we may process:

- **Birth details you provide**, such as an optional name or initial, birth date, birth time, birthplace, and saved chart profiles.
- **Account information**, such as your email address and authentication identifiers, if you choose to create an account when that feature is available.
- **Transaction information** from payment providers if you purchase paid features. We do not receive or store your full payment-card number.
- **Place-search queries**, when you search for a birthplace. Your search text is sent to our server so we can return matching locations and time zones.
- **Technical and usage information**, such as device type, browser, approximate location derived from IP address, pages visited, referral data, and error logs.
- **Communications**, including support requests, feedback, and other messages you send us.

## How We Use Information

We may use information to:

- calculate and display birth charts, transits, and related astrology content;
- provide saved profiles, personalized interpretations, and account features;
- process purchases and manage subscriptions when paid features are offered;
- operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the Service;
- respond to support requests and send service-related messages;
- send optional product communications when you have consented; and
- comply with applicable law and prevent fraud or abuse.

## Birth Data and Browser Storage

Birth dates, times, and locations can be sensitive. We use birth data to provide the chart features you request.

In the current free experience, chart calculations are prepared in your browser, and saved chart profiles are stored locally in your browser unless you choose a future account-sync feature that clearly asks for your consent. We do not sell birth data.

When account-based saving becomes available, the Service will provide a way to delete saved profiles and, where required by law, to export or request deletion of synced data.

## Service Providers

We may use vendors for hosting, authentication, analytics, email delivery, payment processing, geocoding, error monitoring, and automated language generation. They may process information only to provide services to us and are subject to their own legal and security obligations.

For birthplace search, we may use third-party geocoding providers through our internal location API. We use providers that return city coordinates and IANA time zones needed for chart calculation.

## Cookies and Analytics

Annora Sky may use cookies or similar technologies for authentication, preferences, security, and measurement. Where required by law, optional analytics or advertising cookies will be used only after consent.

## Retention and Security

We retain information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, including legal, security, and accounting requirements. We use reasonable safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

## Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or receive a copy of personal information, or to object to certain processing. You may also withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

You can remove charts saved in your browser by deleting them in the Service or by clearing site data in your browser settings.

To make a privacy request, email [support@annorasky.com](mailto:support@annorasky.com). We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

## Children

Annora Sky is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Users under the age of majority in their location should use the Service only with permission from a parent or guardian.

## International Processing

Information may be processed in countries other than the one where you live. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

## Changes

We may update this policy as the Service changes. The revised date will appear at the top of this page.

## Contact

Questions or privacy requests may be sent to [support@annorasky.com](mailto:support@annorasky.com).
